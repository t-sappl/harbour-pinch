# Privacy

Pinch has no account, no tracking, no analytics and no advertising, and it
does not use the internet at all.

It talks only to your earbuds over Bluetooth, through BlueZ on the phone:
it reads their state (noise control, battery, gestures, settings, model,
firmware, serial number) and sends the settings you change.

Data stored on the phone (in the app's settings file): the chosen earbuds
(name, Bluetooth address, model name), the last noise cancellation strength,
whether the earbuds have noise control, the last spatial audio setting and
the app settings. The serial number and firmware version are only shown,
not stored.

Nothing is sent to the developer.
