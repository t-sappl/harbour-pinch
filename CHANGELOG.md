# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release (developed as "Sailbuds" before).

- Noise control: off, transparency, noise cancellation (low, mid, high,
  adaptive); follows the pinch gesture on the buds
- Battery of both earbuds and the case with charging state, also on the cover
- Cover with mode, battery and "next mode"
- Connect the earbuds from the app (pull-down, cover, optionally on start)
- Gestures per side, including the modes "noise control" switches between
- Find my earbuds (ring left/right), ear tip fit test
- Dual connection: switch and device management
- Personalized noise cancellation (Ear (2)); bass boost, spatial audio,
  Super Mic (Ear (3)); in-ear detection, low latency
- Model detection by serial number; info page with model, firmware and serial
- Models without noise control (Ear (open)) show the other settings only
- Over-ear headphones: single battery value
- Device list shows audio devices only
- English and German
